About Yatra 37

The Legacy of Yatra

A heritage of leadership, tradition, and passing the torch since 1990 AD.

Why "Yatra"?

In Sanskrit and Bengali, Yatra signifies a meaningful pilgrimage or journey. At Udaipur English Medium H.S. School, Yatra is not merely an annual celebration; it marks the symbolic transition of the outgoing Class 12 batch from school life into the wider world.

Organised through the collective initiative, management, and artistic direction of the senior students, every edition challenges the graduating class to manage large-scale stage productions, finances, discipline, and audio-visual consoles.

Passing the Torch: On the closing evening, the Class 12 leadership formally entrusts the celebration banner to Class 11, charging them to uphold the institutional standard for Yatra 38.
